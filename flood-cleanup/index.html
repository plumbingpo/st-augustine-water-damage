Floods start in many ways: heavy rain, storm surge, a river over its banks, a dead sump pump, a sewer backup or a major plumbing failure, and all of them call for fast professional help. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. For flood emergencies anywhere in St. Augustine, St. Augustine Restoration is available 24/7 to extract the water, disinfect affected areas and bring the property back safely.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. Materials beyond saving, like contaminated drywall, damaged insulation and saturated carpet pad, are removed and disposed of the right way.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. We check moisture levels every day with calibrated meters and thermal imaging so the drying is complete and mold does not get a start. Your insurance adjuster will want scope-of-work reports, equipment logs, moisture maps and photographs, so we keep that documentation going through the whole project.

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Extend your downspouts to carry rain from the roof out past the foundation
  • Coat basement walls with waterproofing and seal any cracks in the foundation
  • Clear leaves and debris from storm drains and gutters, which lets water drain the way it should
  • Have a backwater valve fitted to the sewer line so sewage cannot flow back inside
  • Make sure the ground slopes away from your foundation on every side
  • Being outside a designated flood zone does not rule out flooding, so think about flood coverage

Signs You Need Flood Cleanup in St. Augustine

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water pooled on any floor of the building, no matter where it came from
  • Seepage at door thresholds, window wells or cracks in the foundation
  • Soaked landscaping that leaves water pooling against your foundation
  • A layer of silt, dirt or debris on the floor after water recedes
  • Basement flooding after the sump pump quit in a downpour
  • Living areas flooded by contaminated water from a backed-up drain or sewer
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through

The Benefits of Professional Flood Cleanup

Flood cleanup is far more than pumping out standing water. Chemicals, bacteria, sewage and other contamination ride in with the flood and create real health dangers. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.
