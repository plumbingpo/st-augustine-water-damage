Our systematic approach delivers quality results on every project. Here's how we handle water damage repair from start to finish:

1

Confirm It Is Dry

We take final moisture readings on framing, subfloor and remaining drywall. Reconstruction only begins once those numbers are at normal levels for the material, so nothing damp gets sealed in.

Warning Signs for Water Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Door casings and baseboards have warped or pulled off the wall surface.
  • Walking over the formerly wet area, you feel soft spots in the floor.
  • The lower part of cabinet sides or bases feels soft, looks swollen or is peeling.
  • After drying, the hardwood is still cupped, crowned or lifting.
  • Where water ran down a wall, the paint has blistered, peeled or turned brown.
  • A door frame or slab swelled, so the door rubs or fails to close.
  • The edges of your laminate or vinyl planks have puffed up, curled or pulled apart.

How to Prevent Water Damage Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • No wall or floor should be sealed by any contractor until you have seen final moisture readings.
  • Save spare tile and flooring, plus paint color details, for matching later repairs.
  • Once a year, renew the caulk at sinks, showers and tubs to keep water from entering the wall.
  • Ventilate with the bathroom exhaust fan while showering and afterward to protect new drywall.
  • Store pictures of the finished work alongside the scope for future reference.
  • In case the insurance company asks down the road, photograph anything damaged while it is still in place.
